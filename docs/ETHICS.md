# Ethics, provenance, and publication boundaries

## Data rule

All examples must be invented from scratch for this project. Do not use actual
client material, session notes, recordings, supervision examples, personal
relationship disclosures, internet relationship posts, or private messages.
Changing names, removing identifiers, obtaining a casual permission, making a
composite, or rewriting an actual case does not satisfy this rule.
Do not use excluded material even as an intermediate draft.

The v0.1 fixtures were created from generic fictional situations on 2026-10-24.
No clinical files, messages, or actual cases were used. This is a provenance
statement about the construction process, not a guarantee that an invented
scenario cannot resemble someone's experience. All gold labels remain provisional
until an independent reviewer has reviewed them.

For each new contribution record: authorship, date, construction
method, source declaration, independent reviewer, known concerns, and a specific
attestation that it is not drawn from a real person. Metadata checks can enforce
the declaration's presence; software cannot verify its truth.

## Scope and harm

Only fictional adults are in scope. No diagnosis, crisis intervention, therapy
recommendation, real-world dispute resolution, or scoring of actual people.
Do not market high scores as proof of clinical safety, empathetic experience, or
readiness for emotional-support deployment. Do not optimize for persuading users
that an AI understands them or should replace human support.

Validation concerns the intelligibility of a reported feeling. It does not make
every belief accurate, every action acceptable, or both parties equally
responsible. The blocked-exit case tests this distinction with an explicit
non-graphic harmful act. It is not a clinical or legal classification exercise.
Additional sensitive content needs a documented research purpose and reviewer
choice to skip it. Report skipped and missing ratings; never silently impute them.

There are no research participants in this engineering pilot. Before recruiting
people for a study of AI's effects or collecting personal disclosures, seek an
appropriate independent ethics review and design consent, withdrawal, data
minimization, and support procedures. Do not describe this document as IRB
approval or a determination that review is unnecessary.

## Public transparency with boundaries

Publish fictional prompts, protocol versions, model identifiers and settings,
synthetic outputs, annotation instructions, aggregate results, and error logs
after review. Publish anonymized reviewer identifiers only with agreement.
Never publish credentials, account inventories, private account metadata,
transport headers, or personal information. Raw model output can itself invent
personal details; review it before publication. Mark any redaction and why it
occurred rather than quietly changing a record.

If sensitive material is accidentally submitted, stop processing and do not
quote or copy it into another public artifact. Ask the maintainer privately to
remove it and consider repository history and cached copies. Public deletion
cannot guarantee erasure. Do not post the sensitive text in a bug report. No
private reporting channel is configured by this scaffold; maintainers must
establish one before accepting sensitive incident reports.
